1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OBJECTIVE
================================================================================
Small retail shops in local communities often rely on physical notebooks, paper 
cash memos, and mental arithmetic. This causes significant issues:
  1. Billing arithmetic mistakes (miscalculated discounts and taxes).
  2. Untracked customer credit / due balances (Khata records lost or disputed).
  3. Stock mismatch and running out of essential grocery staples unexpectedly.
  4. Undetected product expirations leading to financial loss and customer churn.

"APNA BAZAR" replaces these manual paper-based processes with an easy-to-use, 
lightweight, web-based digital inventory and point-of-sale billing application. 
The system was designed following the philosophy of a realistic, high-quality 
student project: transparent code, simple CRUD operations, zero over-engineering,
and an elegant, distraction-free monochrome (black-and-white) user interface.

5. DATABASE LIFECYCLE & BUSINESS RULES
================================================================================

RULE 1: INWARD PURCHASE INCREMENT
  Whenever a purchase is recorded from a wholesale vendor:
    db.products.update_one({'_id': product_id}, {'$inc': {'quantity': purchased_qty}})
  Stock immediately increases.

RULE 2: SALES CHECKOUT DECREMENT
  Whenever an invoice is completed:
    db.products.update_one({'_id': product_id}, {'$inc': {'quantity': -sold_qty}})
  Stock immediately decreases. If sold_qty > available stock, checkout is blocked.

RULE 3: SALE RETURN / CANCELLATION RESTITUTION
  Whenever a sale is cancelled:
    db.products.update_one({'_id': product_id}, {'$inc': {'quantity': sold_qty}})
  The sold items are returned to stock. The invoice is marked as 'Cancelled' 
  (never permanently deleted, ensuring audit compliance).

RULE 4: MATHEMATICAL BILLING FORMULAS
  Item Total          = Quantity * Selling Price
  Subtotal            = Sum of all Item Totals
  Discounted Subtotal = Subtotal - Discount
  Tax (GST)           = (Discounted Subtotal * Tax Rate %) / 100
  Grand Total         = Discounted Subtotal + Tax
  Due Amount          = Grand Total - Amount Paid by Customer

6. COLLEGE VIVA PRESENTATION & DEFENSE GUIDE
================================================================================

Q1: What is the primary purpose of your Community Engagement Project?
A1: To assist small, unorganized local shopkeepers (such as Apna Bazar) in transitioning 
    from manual paper khatas and cash memos to an error-free, automated inventory 
    and billing management system that tracks real-time stock, customer dues, and profit.

Q2: Why did you choose Python Flask instead of Django or Node.js?
A2: Flask is a minimalist, flexible microframework that avoids enterprise boilerplate. 
    It allows clear, readable Python functions for routing, easy integration with PyMongo, 
    and straightforward session handling without unnecessary bloat.

Q3: How is customer data and inventory stored in MongoDB?
A3: Data is stored in JSON-like BSON documents across clean collections: products, 
    categories, suppliers, purchases, customers, sales, stock_movements, and settings. 
    Sales invoices store embedded item arrays, which naturally aligns with document-oriented NoSQL.

Q4: How did you verify that stock updates and calculations are 100% accurate?
A4: Through an automated unit test suite (`test_app.py`). It specifically executes 
    the complete stock cycle: starting with 10 units, purchasing 5 (verified 15), 
    selling 3 (verified 12), and cancelling the sale (verified restored back to 15), 
    as well as exact subtotal, tax, discount, and due balance formulas.
